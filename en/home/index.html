About

Spectrum Films is a boutique organization with experienced professionals who will drive your project from beginning to end and offer you the best logistical solutions to suit your production. Our services are personal and dedicated.

We help you from research to wrap, to minimize the odds of shooting in a foreign country. From white sandy beaches with turquoise blue seas, to jungle or desert, Spectrum will always find the scenery that can best serve the most sophisticated productions. We also offer experienced bilingual crews, trained to work in quality productions. In Spectrum Films you will find the perfect combination of skillful people and the right locations at favorable rates.

Our main goal is to ensure that your shoot runs smoothly. In all these years we have produced features, commercials, web content and documentaries for national and international clients. Spectrum welcomes you for your next shoot in Mexico!

PRODUCER

Concepción (Conchita) Taboada has worked in the production of foreign films in Mexico and has offered production services since the 1980s. She has produced many Mexican films and countless commercials for advertising campaigns around the world, establishing a good reputation for quality and creativity in production.
